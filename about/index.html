WHO AM I

Hello, I'm Pranavi Rudradev, a Marketing major and Psychology minor (BBA) from FLAME University.

I've always been curious about people, what catches our attention, shapes our choices, and makes certain things stay with us? These questions are also what draw me to brands. There's something fascinating about looking beyond the obvious and discovering the little things that shape how people think, feel, and connect with the world around them.

A few things that make me, me.

Water feels like home.

Swimming has been a meaningful part of my life, including representing my state in competitions. I love consuming and being in the water, and feel equally drawn to the outdoors.

Occasionally, the clouds have my attention.

I can get distracted by a good sky, an interesting shadow, or the way sunlight falls on something ordinary. I love being outdoors, exploring, and finding reasons to take the longer way around.

I think I can befriend any animal.

This confidence may be my biggest red flag. So far, optimism remains undefeated.

Coffee connoisseur. Questionable lyricist.

I love coffee and singing. My commitment to the melody is usually stronger than my commitment to the actual lyrics.

HOW I THINK.

I start with curiosity, explore what could be true, strip away what doesn't fit, and arrive at the simplest, most meaningful explanation, one that actually helps us move forward.

“The simplest explanation is usually the right one.”

— OCCAM’S RAZOR
Not the simplest idea, but the simplest that explains the most.
1.

ABDUCTIVE
REASONING

FROM OBSERVATION
TO POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS.
Notice something intersting (or unexpected).

What could explain this?

What else might be going on?

Are there deeper reasons?

Could there be a different perspective?

I start with an observation and explore multiple plausible explanations, rather than jumping to the obvious one.

2.

ELIMINATION
(USING OCCAM’S RAZOR)

TESTING, NARROWING,
AND FINDING THE SIMPLEST
PLAUSIBLE EXPLANATION.
  1. 1. List the possibilities.
  2. 2. Test against evidence
       (data, research, human behaviour),
  3. 3. Eliminate what doesn't fit.
  4. 4. Apply Occam's Razor
       prefer the simplest explanation that accounts for the most.

Keep what explains the most with the fewest assumptions.

3.

INSIGHT
& DIRECTION

FROM A SIMPLER EXPLANATION
TO STRATEGIC POSSIBILITIES.
A clearer understanding that leads to better questions, sharper strategy and more meaningful ideas.

The result is a clearer, more meaningful understanding that can inform strategy, creative directions, or further exploration.

MY THINKING PROCESS, STEP BY STEP.
01

OBSERVE

Notice behaviours, patterns, contradictions or anything that catches my attention.

02

QUESTION

Ask what could be driving this. Challenge the obvious explanation.

03

EXPLORE

Generate multiple plausible explanations (abductive reasoning).

04

ELIMINATE

Test each possibility against evidence and strip away what doesn't fit.

05

SIMPLIFY
(OCCAM’S RAZOR)

Prefer the simplest explanation that accounts for the most.

06

APPLY

Turn the clearest insight into strategic directions and creative possibilities.
